Step 1 — Scan a QR

Give camera permission and point at any QR. Its text decides which animation to play.

If the camera doesn’t open, make sure you’re on HTTPS and using a supported mobile browser (Chrome on Android).

Step 2 — Place the Model

Move your phone to find a surface. Tap on the surface to place the 3D model. The animation will auto‑play.

Tip: well‑lit, textured surfaces (floor/table) are easier to detect.
